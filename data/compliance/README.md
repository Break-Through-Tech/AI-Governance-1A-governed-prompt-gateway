# Output compliance seed fixtures

24 synthetic author-labeled cases covering grounding, claimed actions, fictional
context, contacts, identifiers, completion, format, and recommendations. Labels
are drafts requiring independent human review. No real customer data is included.

The first 12 scenario families are development cases and the remaining 12 are
held out. Keep paraphrases of a family in the same split. Expand to the plan's
240-case target and obtain independent labels before reporting release accuracy.
Do not treat the offline fixture-validation report as a model benchmark.
